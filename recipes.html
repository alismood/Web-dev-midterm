Spaghetti Carbonara

  • Boil pasta in salted water.
  • Fry guanciale until crispy.
  • Mix eggs and pecorino cheese.
  • Combine pasta, pork, and egg mixture off the heat.

Bavette Pasta Carbonara

  • Cook bavette pasta al dente.
  • Prepare a rich cream and bacon base.
  • Toss pasta in the sauce.
  • Top with black pepper.

Bacon and Cream Pasta

  • Render bacon fat in a skillet.
  • Add heavy cream and simmer.
  • Stir in cooked pasta.
  • Garnish with parmesan.

Healthy Veggie Bowl

  • Roast sweet potatoes and broccoli.
  • Cook quinoa.
  • Assemble with fresh spinach.
  • Drizzle with tahini dressing.

Classic Margherita Pizza

  • Stretch pizza dough into a circle.
  • Spread crushed tomatoes evenly.
  • Add fresh mozzarella slices.
  • Bake at max heat until crust is blistered.

Smash Burger

  • Form ground beef into loose balls.
  • Smash flat on a hot griddle.
  • Add cheese and let melt.
  • Serve on a toasted brioche bun.

Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken

  • Marinate chicken in lemon juice and herbs.
  • Preheat grill to medium-high.
  • Grill 6-8 minutes per side.
  • Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Italian Meatballs

  • Mix ground beef, pork, breadcrumbs, and egg.
  • Form into 2-inch balls.
  • Brown in a skillet.
  • Simmer in marinara sauce until cooked through.

Summer Fruit Salad

  • Dice strawberries, kiwi, and mango.
  • Toss with blueberries and grapes.
  • Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top.
  • Garnish with mint leaves.
